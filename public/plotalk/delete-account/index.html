Delete your account

PloTalk: AI Character Chat, by Samuel Nduka - ADONISTECH

In the app

  1. Open PloTalk and go to Profile.
  2. Open Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away.

Without the app

Email samonduka19@gmail.com from the email address of the Apple or Google account you use to sign in to PloTalk, with the subject "Delete my PloTalk account". We confirm and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

Your profile, chats, memories, created characters and their portraits, story progress, gems, reports, subscription records, notification settings, devices and notification history are permanently deleted from our servers.

What is kept

Records we are legally required to keep (such as accounting records) are kept for as long as the law requires. Purchase records that Apple, Google and RevenueCat must keep are kept by them under their own policies. Usage analytics, if you allowed them, are pseudonymous and expire under the retention period set at PostHog.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel PloTalk Plus. Cancel it on iPhone in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or on Android in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
