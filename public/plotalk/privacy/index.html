What we collect

  • Account data. When you sign in with Apple (or Google, where offered) we receive an account identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email address (with Apple this can be a private relay address). We also record that you confirmed you are 18 or older, and when.
  • Age check. The birth year you enter during onboarding is checked on your device to confirm you are an adult. It is not sent to our servers.
  • Profile and preferences. Your display name, pronouns, role, avatar choice, story preferences and app settings.
  • Your content. Messages you send to characters and their replies, images you choose to share in a chat, story progress and decisions, what characters remember about you (memories), favourites, and characters you create, including any portrait photo you upload for them.
  • Purchases. Whether you have PloTalk Plus, its status, trial and renewal dates, and your gem purchases and balance. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Usage needed to run the service. How many AI messages you send per day (to apply plan limits and prevent abuse), and technical records of each AI request (time, model, size, success or error).
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications on your iPhone, we store a push token for your device (issued by Apple through Expo), the permission status, your time zone and app version, so we can deliver reminders at a suitable local time. We also record which notifications were sent to you and whether you opened them, and your notification choices, including whether and when you agreed to news about new content.
  • Usage analytics (optional). Only if you allow it in the app: which screens you open, buttons you tap, onboarding steps, characters and chapters you start, purchases started or completed, reply times and errors. Never your chats, what you write, what characters reply, your name or email. See "Usage analytics" below.
  • Reports. If you report an AI reply, we store the report: your account identifier, the character, the reported text and the reason you chose.

PloTalk does not show ads, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Usage analytics are optional and off until you allow them. The app asks for access to your photo library only when you pick an image, and only the image you choose is used.

How AI is used

Character replies, story progression and the optional AI writing help are generated by Anthropic, our AI provider. To create a reply, PloTalk sends Anthropic what you write in the chat, any image you share there, and the character, story context and memories needed for that reply. Before anything you write is sent to Anthropic for the first time, the app asks for your permission. You can withdraw it at any time in Profile → Privacy & safety → Share chats with AI; characters then cannot reply until you allow it again. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models.

All characters and stories are fictional and written by AI. Replies can be inaccurate and are never professional advice.

Notifications

PloTalk asks for permission to send notifications only after you have used the app, never on first launch. If you allow them, we may send reminders about a story you started, your first conversation, a character you began creating, your daily messages being restored (PloTalk Plus) or time away from the app. We send at most one a day and three a week, in the evening of your time zone, and we pause them if you ignore several in a row. Notifications never show what you or a character wrote, and characters never message you through them. Notifications about newly published characters, stories and collections are sent only if you separately agree to them. You can choose each category, withdraw that agreement or turn notifications off in Profile → Notifications, and turn them off completely in your iPhone's Settings. When you sign out on a device, it stops receiving notifications for your account.

Usage analytics

If you allow it in the app, PloTalk records how the app is used, as listed above. The data is linked to a pseudonymous identifier (a one-way hash of your account id), processed by PostHog in the European Union on our behalf, used only to improve PloTalk, and never sold or used for advertising or tracking across apps. You can switch it off at any time in Profile → Privacy & safety → Usage analytics. App Store download statistics come from Apple in aggregated form.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide PloTalk (accounts, chats, stories, memories, sync across your devices, subscriptions and gems): performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To send your chat content to our AI provider: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To send reminders about your stories and account after you allowed notifications: our legitimate interest in helping you continue what you started (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object at any time by turning them off. News about new content and usage analytics: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep PloTalk safe (usage limits, abuse prevention, handling reports, security): our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • To meet legal obligations, such as accounting and tax records: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

Who receives your data

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage. Data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Anthropic: generates AI replies, as described above.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions and in-app purchases; receives your PloTalk user identifier and purchase records from Apple.
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.): delivers push notifications to Apple; receives your device's push token and the notification text.
  • PostHog: usage analytics, only with your consent; data is stored in the EU.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store payments and the Apple Push Notification service. Google: sign-in, only if you choose it.

These providers process data on our behalf under their data processing terms. Anthropic, RevenueCat and Expo are based in the United States; transfers outside the EU/EEA rely on the safeguards in those terms, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, chats, memories, created characters, story progress, gems, reports, subscription records, notification settings, devices and notification history from our servers. Purchase records that Apple and RevenueCat must keep for accounting are kept by them under their own policies, and we keep records we are legally required to keep for as long as the law requires.

The record of notifications sent to you is kept for 180 days, and records of opens, permission changes and consent changes for up to 400 days; your notification choices are kept while your account exists; a device's push token is deleted when you sign out on it, when Apple reports it is no longer valid, or when you delete your account. Usage analytics events are kept by PostHog under the retention period we set there.

Deleting your account does not cancel a PloTalk Plus subscription, which is billed by Apple. Cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Chats are also kept on your device. You can clear them in Profile → Privacy & safety → Clear chat history, and deleting the app removes them from the device.

Adults only

PloTalk is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor uses PloTalk, contact us and we will delete the account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier processing. Write to samonduka19@gmail.com; we answer within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in Slovakia the Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky (dataprotection.gov.sk), or the authority where you live.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Your sign-in session is stored in the iOS Keychain, and access to your data on our servers is restricted to your own account by database access rules.

Changes

We will publish any changes on this page and update the date above. For material changes we will also let you know in the app.
