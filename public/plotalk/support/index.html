Frequently asked questions

What is PloTalk Plus?

PloTalk Plus is an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription that lets you send messages to characters, progress stories and use AI writing help. Eligible new subscribers get a free trial; the App Store shows the exact price, trial and renewal terms before you confirm. Without Plus you can still browse Discover, search, view character profiles and read your past chats.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → PloTalk and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge. You can also open this screen from the PloTalk Plus page in the app.

How do I restore my purchases?

Sign in with the same account, open Profile → PloTalk Plus and tap Restore purchases at the bottom.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report an inappropriate reply?

In a chat, long-press the reply and choose Report, or open the ⋯ menu and choose Report latest reply. We review every report. You can also email us.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and its data from our servers. It does not cancel an Apple subscription, so cancel that first in Settings.

How do I stop sharing my chats with AI?

Open Profile → Privacy & safety and turn off Share chats with AI. Characters can't reply until you turn it back on. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Who can use PloTalk?

PloTalk is for adults aged 18 and over. All characters and stories are fictional and written by AI.
